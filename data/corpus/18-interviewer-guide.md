# Northwind Analytics — Guide for Interviewers

The hiring document describes the loop from the candidate's side. This one is
instructions for the people running it.

## Before the interview

Read the candidate's résumé and the previous stage's written feedback. Arrive
with your questions decided. An interviewer who improvises produces feedback
that cannot be compared across candidates, which is the single biggest source of
noise in the loop.

## Running the practical exercise

Give the ticket, state that the ambiguity is intentional, and then stay quiet.
Answer direct questions honestly and completely. Do not hint. The strongest
signal in the exercise is what the candidate does in the first ten minutes
before writing any code.

## Calibration

Every interviewer shadows two loops and is shadowed on two before interviewing
alone. Interviewers are recalibrated if their hire rate drifts more than 20
percentage points from the panel average over 10 interviews.

## Writing feedback

Feedback is submitted within 24 hours and before reading anyone else's. Write
evidence, then a conclusion — never the reverse. A rating with no quoted
behaviour behind it is discounted at debrief.

## In the debrief

The weakest-signal interviewer speaks first, the hiring manager last. Ties are
not resolved by seniority; an unresolved tie is a no-hire, because the cost of a
mis-hire at Northwind has been measured at roughly nine months of team
throughput.

## What interviewers must not ask

No questions about age, family status, visa history beyond work authorisation,
current compensation, or anything a candidate's public social media suggests.
Compensation is set by level and band, so asking is both illegal in several of
our hubs and useless.
