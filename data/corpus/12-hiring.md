# Northwind Analytics — Engineering Hiring

## The loop

The engineering loop is four stages: a 30-minute screen, a 90-minute practical
exercise on a real codebase, a systems-design conversation, and a values
interview. There is no whiteboard algorithm round — it was removed in 2023 after
two years of data showed no correlation with on-the-job performance.

## The practical exercise

Candidates work in a prepared repository with a failing test and an ambiguous
ticket. The ambiguity is the point: the signal is whether the candidate asks
about it or guesses. Candidates may use any tooling, including AI assistants,
and are asked to narrate what they accept and what they reject.

## Levels

Engineering has five levels, E1 to E5, plus two staff-plus tracks. The
difference between E3 and E4 is scope of ambiguity, not years or output: an E4
is handed a problem, an E3 is handed a solution to build.

## Decision

Hiring decisions are made in a single debrief within two business days of the
final interview. Interviewers submit written feedback before the debrief and
cannot edit it after seeing others' — a rule added because feedback drift toward
the loudest voice was measurable.

## Offers and onboarding

Offers include the level, the band and where in the band the offer sits, and
why. Start dates are set so that the new hire's onboarding buddy is not on-call
that week.
